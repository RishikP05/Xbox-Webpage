About

The Xbox Series X is the more powerful console in the Series family. It is designed for players who want high performance, fast loading, and features such as 4K gaming.

The Xbox Series S is smaller and designed as a more compact, all-digital option. It supports modern Xbox games while taking up less space.

Both consoles use fast SSD storage, support modern Xbox features, and can play a large selection of games. The main differences are size, performance, storage, and disc support.

Xbox Series Features

  • Fast SSD storage
  • Quick Resume
  • High-resolution gaming
  • Up to 120 FPS support
  • Backward compatibility
